Machaon

Named for the healer at Troy, son of Asclepius. One mark, one blue, one typeface, and the old art of medicine in halftone.

The mark

A stethoscope

A bold white stroke on a blue tile, no text, no outline. Clear space equals the stroke width. Smallest size 16 px.

Mascot

Aski

The serpent from Asclepius' rod, the snake still on every medical sign, coiled into a friendly S. Flat blue, a pale belly line, no outline. Use it for empty states, the 404 and anything that needs a face.
