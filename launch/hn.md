Show HN: Machaon – a dose log that warns you before things overlap

## Body
I take more than one supplement and could never picture how they overlapped in time, which is exactly where interactions matter, not just what I took on the same calendar day. Machaon logs a dose in two taps, derives your "active stack" from duration windows so it's always correct for right now, and checks a new entry against everything still active before it saves. Underneath that is a library of 200 substances with harm-reduction notes I wrote by hand, plus daily check-ins, a heatmap of patterns, and lab results in and out of range.

It's web, iOS, and macOS, built on Supabase for auth and storage (row-level security per user), no analytics or trackers. Free to use, with a $1 one-time CSV export on web. Would appreciate feedback on the interaction checker in particular.
